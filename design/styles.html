Types of MI › Overview

Myocardial infarction

Death of heart muscle from prolonged ischemia, classified by cause into five types.

Background ✓ read

Defined by a troponin rise above the 99th centile with a rise or fall, plus evidence of ischemia.

Pathophysiology go deeper →

Type 1 is plaque rupture with thrombus; type 2 is a supply–demand mismatch without rupture.

Presentation go deeper →

Pressure-like chest pain over 20 minutes, often with sweating, nausea or breathlessness.

Diagnosis go deeper →

Serial high-sensitivity troponin and an ECG within 10 minutes of arrival.
